Write what matters. Inkwright writes the rest.

Take notes by hand or by keyboard in any meeting, class or call. Afterwards Inkwright fills in what was said, beside your own words, with the moment each line came from. No bot joins the call, and it can all run on your device.

A meeting, start to finish.

Inkwright knows your calendar, listens while you write, and turns a few rough lines into notes you can send.

Before in your calendar

Coming-up meetings with their join links. Start notes in a tap, with the attendees and a template that fits. A short brief from your earlier meetings with the same people: what was decided, what’s still open, what to ask.

During on paper or keys

Write what matters to you. Inkwright records the room, or both sides of a Zoom, Meet or Teams call, with no bot in the meeting. Lost the thread? Ask what was just said.

After in a minute

Your notes, complete: your lines kept word for word, the details beneath them in grey, every one linked to the moment it was said. Action items with owners. Ask for changes, or send it on.

A notebook first. It feels like paper.

Ink under the nib before the next frame
Pressure, tilt and palm rejection on Apple Pencil, S Pen and Windows pens. Scribble to erase.
Answers in ink
Circle a question and the answer is written beside it, in a colour never mistaken for yours. Enhanced notes can go onto the page the same way, in a hand matched to yours.
Notes that listen
Tap any word you wrote to hear what was being said at that moment.
Study and maths
Handwritten maths solved, flashcards from any note, and a tutor that quizzes you on what you wrote.
Every page
Ruled, dot, Cornell and planner paper, typed documents, and PDFs to annotate, fill in and sign.

Private by design, not by promise.

No bot in your meetings
Inkwright records from your own computer or phone. Nobody else sees a notetaker join.
AI on your device
Transcription, enhanced notes, recipes and briefs can all run on the device, offline. Or bring your own key for any provider.
Delete audio after transcribing
Keep the transcript and your notes, drop the recording, if you prefer.
Your files, your storage
Notes are plain files on your devices, synced through your own Drive, WebDAV or folder. No account, no server of ours.
One checkpoint for every AI call
Keep a notebook off-limits, mask emails and numbers before a model sees them, and check a signed record of what was sent.

Your notebook is a server for your other tools.

MCP for Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor; A2A; a REST API with OpenAPI; signed webhooks; a typed SDK and a CLI. Meetings and notes become context for every agent you use.

# Give Claude Code your notebook
claude mcp add inkwright -- node apps/mcp-server/dist/index.js \
  --vault ~/Notes --read-only

# Or call it like any web API
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY" \
  "localhost:8765/api/v1/search?q=pricing&mode=hybrid"

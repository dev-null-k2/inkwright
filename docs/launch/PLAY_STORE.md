**App name (30):** Inkwright: handwriting notes

**Short description (80):** Notes that feel like paper. An AI that answers in ink. Your files, your AI.

**Full description (≤ 4000):**

Inkwright is a handwriting notebook for people who think with a pen.

WRITES LIKE PAPER
• Ink appears under the nib instantly, with a native ink tip on Android
• Soft nib-on-paper sound and haptic paper grain
• Scribble over a word to erase it; press the pen button for the eraser
• Fountain, calligraphy, pencil, brush and highlighter pens; pressure and tilt
• Ruled, dot, Cornell, music and planner paper; fill in and sign any PDF

THINKS WITH YOU, ON THE PAGE
• Lasso a question and the AI answers beside it, in handwriting
• Draw a grid to get a table, circle an equation to solve and graph it, circle a date to add it to your calendar
• Record a lecture and tap your ink to hear what was being said
• Your notes become a wiki where every sentence cites where it came from
• Flashcards from any note, and a tutor that quizzes you

YOURS
• Use your own AI key (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini and more) or private on-device models
• Notes are plain files. Sync through your own Google Drive, WebDAV or a folder
• No account with us, no ads, no tracking
• Mark notebooks as "never leaves this device"
• Open source (Apache-2.0)

FOR POWER USERS
• Every feature can be switched off, with presets for students, writers, developers and researchers
• Your notebook can serve Claude, Cursor and other AI tools through MCP, plus a REST API

**Category:** Productivity · **Content rating:** Everyone · **Data safety:** no data collected by the developer. Notes are stored on the device and in storage the user chooses. AI requests go only to the provider the user configures. See docs/PRIVACY.md.

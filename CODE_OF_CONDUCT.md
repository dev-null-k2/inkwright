# Code of conduct

Inkwright follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:
- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- No harassment of any kind.

To report a problem, email the maintainer privately through the address on [github.com/dev-null-k2](https://github.com/dev-null-k2). Reports are handled confidentially.
